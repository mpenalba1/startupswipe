The closed beta is now on Android too

A day after iPhone, the closed beta of StartupSwipe is open on Android, through Google Play.

It’s the same chapter I as on iPhone, with today’s fixes.

If you’re in the group, you get a link to join. Open it on your Android with the Google account you gave us, tap Become a tester, and install the game from Google Play. If something breaks or doesn’t make sense, send us a screenshot.
