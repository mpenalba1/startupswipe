The closed beta is open on iPhone

Augusto already has the garage and the twenty-four thousand dollars. What he needed was someone to try it before anyone else.

Starting today, a small group can play chapter I of StartupSwipe on iPhone, through TestFlight. It’s the whole chapter, start to finish.

If you’re in the group, the invitation comes from TestFlight. If something breaks or doesn’t make sense, open TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback. A screenshot helps.

News about the beta will be posted here.
